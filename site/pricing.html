Read eligibility ↗

Before you choose a plan.

Does local AI SUS need a subscription?

No. The editor experience, maps, freshness checks, and local PR check are free. Generating or refreshing with an agent uses that agent’s account and allowance.

What does Cloud receive?

Only opted-in maps, sidecars, check results, and related metadata. These can include repository names, paths, descriptions, and commit details. Source files are not uploaded. Read the data details.

Can someone view a shared map without a seat?

Yes. Read-only viewers of shared maps and PR pages are free. Team seats apply to members who manage or upload to a workspace.

Who qualifies for education pricing?

Verified school and university teams, including course projects. An institution-issued email or a manual review can establish eligibility. The 50% discount is renewed yearly.

Is there a free Cloud tier?

Yes. It includes unlimited public repositories, one private repository, and 30 days of history. Local use is still available without Cloud.

Do dashboards measure individual developers?

No. Team dashboards report repository-level architecture health. They do not rank or score individual developers.
